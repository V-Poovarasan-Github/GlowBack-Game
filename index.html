The 4-Step Rivalry Loop

How Asynchronous Duels Work

No matchmaking lobbies required. Settle the score through pure deterministic codes.

1

Share Code

Reach a score of 5 or higher in Solo mode, then send your generated Challenge Code to a friend. It locks in your exact sequence and target score.

2x2-5-T3E-ALEX-DG9A-Q579D-B
2

Friend Plays

Your friend pastes the code in Glowback and plays the exact same pad sequence seed within the allowed tries to beat your target score.

Target: 5 · 3 Tries · Same Seed
3

Send Reply

When their tries finish, Glowback mints a tamper-proof Reply Code containing their final score so they can send it right back to you.

2x2-6-R3E-HERO-DG9A-Q579D-K
4

Settle Score

Paste their Reply Code in your Rivalry tab to log the Win, Loss, or Tie — then tap Revenge or Send Challenge to keep the feud alive.
