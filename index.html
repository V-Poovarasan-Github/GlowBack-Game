What's actually in the box

Built to be memorized, not metered

Every system in GlowBack — from real-time audio synthesis to deterministic seed codes — runs locally with zero external dependencies.

Four grids, two difficulties, two paces

Play 2×2, 3×3, 4×4, or 5×4 (5 rows × 4 columns). Choose Easy's cumulative recall or Hard's fresh sequence every round, at Classic or accelerating Progressive speed.

Real synthesized tone per pad

Each pad synthesizes its own pure PCM musical frequency with psychoacoustic harmonics on-device in real time via Android AudioTrack — zero static audio files.

Haptics that mean something

Distinct tactile vibration waveforms mapped to pad taps, round clears, errors, and victory milestones — with one-tap cycling between Sound, Vibrate, and Mute.

Head-to-head rivalry cards

Each friend gets a dedicated card tracking lifetime Wins, Losses, Ties, and scrollable per-grid breakdowns — complete with Revenge, Tie-Breaker, and try-preserving Resume.

Reminders you control

Opt-in local daily nudges via Android WorkManager highlight unplayed rival challenges or personal streaks without ever touching a network server.

Pass-and-play & zero login

Install and play immediately in Solo or 2P/3P/4P local elimination on a single phone — no accounts, no sign-in walls, and no internet permission.

The part that replaces a server

A duel fits in one line of text

Every challenge and reply is a compact 7-segment deterministic string. The same code always rebuilds the exact pad sequence on your rival's device and verifies integrity locally in 0ms.

4x4-6-T3E-ALEX-DG9A-Q579D-B
4x4 1. Grid Board layout (2×2, 3×3, 4×4, or 5×4).
6 2. Target Target score to beat (or reply score).
T3E 3. Tries · Diff T-Code (or R-Code), 3 tries, Easy mode.
ALEX 4. Sender Callsign of the player who minted the code.
DG9A 5. Chain ID Links outbound challenges to incoming replies.
Q579D 6. Seed Deterministic PRNG seed for the pad sequence.
B 7. Checksum Tamper-proof character blocking manual edits.

Privacy, plainly

Your scores never leave your phone — the codes you choose to send are the only thing that does.

No network permissionGlowBack can't reach the internet even if it wanted to — the permission isn't in the app.
No analytics, no adsNothing is measured, tracked, or sold. There's no SDK for it to go through.
Storage stays localRivals, records, and settings live in on-device storage only, excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.
