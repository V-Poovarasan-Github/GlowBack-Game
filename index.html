What's actually in the box

Built to be memorized, not metered

Four grids, two difficulties, two paces

2×2 through 5×4, Easy's cumulative recall or Hard's fresh sequence every round, at a steady Classic tempo or an accelerating Progressive one. Sixteen ways to play the same core loop.

Real synthesized tone per pad

Each pad plays its own sine-wave frequency, generated on the device in real time — not a sample file.

Haptics that mean something

Distinct vibration waveforms for a tap, a streak, a loss, and a win — felt, not just seen.

A rivalry, not a leaderboard

No global ranking, no server to climb. Each rival card is its own private head-to-head: lifetime wins, losses, ties, and a per-grid breakdown you build up one duel at a time. Tie-breakers, revenge rematches, and a resume for the match you had to step away from are all built in.

Reminders you control

Opt-in daily nudges about unplayed challenges, scheduled locally — never on by default.

Nothing to log into

No account, no email, no password — install it and you're already playing.

The part that replaces a server

A duel fits in one line of text

Every challenge and reply is a short deterministic string. The same code always rebuilds the same sequence on the other device — that's the entire multiplayer layer.

Privacy, plainly

Your scores never leave your phone — the codes you choose to send are the only thing that does.

No network permissionGlowBack can't reach the internet even if it wanted to — the permission isn't in the app.
No analytics, no adsNothing is measured, tracked, or sold. There's no SDK for it to go through.
Storage stays localRivals, records, and settings live in on-device storage only, excluded from cloud backup and device transfer.
